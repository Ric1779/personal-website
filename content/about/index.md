I’m Richards, a Deep Learning engineer driven by a desire to understand and bring structure to the mysterious world we inhabit. My interests naturally gravitated toward physics, mathematics, and computers because each offers a different lens for reasoning about reality, uncovering patterns, and understanding how complex systems emerge and interact. I’m fascinated not only by solving problems but by thinking from first principles: how humans developed mathematics, how logic arises from the mind, how systems give rise to useful applications, and how machines can learn to perceive and reason about the world.

I hold a master’s degree in Image Analysis and ML from Uppsala University and currently work as an **AI & 3D Software Developer** at IKEA, where my interests in DL, computer vision, and CGI come together. More recently, my focus has expanded toward building systems at the intersection of AI agents and operational reality. But my curiosity for visual worlds started long before tech. Growing up, as an army brat, I moved across India and spending much of my time sketching, watching films and anime, and imagining stories, characters, and worlds of my own. That creative instinct still shapes how I think today, and draws me to visual computing.

Outside of work, I enjoy football, powerlifting, and calisthenics. I see physical training as a way to build both discipline and awareness. I also read and reflect on philosophy and psychology, especially ideas around meaning and how humans try to understand reality. Over time, I’ve come to see purpose less as something given and more as something we actively shape through curiosity, creativity, relationships, and the ongoing pursuit of understanding.

At my core, I’m someone who simply wants to keep learning, about the world, about people, and about the strange experience of being alive within all of this.
